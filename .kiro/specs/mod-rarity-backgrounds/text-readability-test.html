🎨 Text Readability Test - Mod Rarity Gradients

Testing Instructions

  • Check if all text is readable at different positions on each gradient
  • Verify that white text has sufficient contrast on light gradient areas
  • Check if yellow stars are visible on yellow/amber gradients (5-star)
  • Ensure badges and labels are readable
  • Test at different screen brightness levels

✅ Readability Checklist

  • White text readable on dark areas (top of gradients) ✓
  • White text readable on light areas (bottom of gradients) - CHECK
  • Yellow stars visible on all gradients - CHECK 5-STAR
  • Badges (symbol, tolerance) have sufficient contrast ✓
  • Small text (11-12px) readable at all positions - CHECK
  • Text shadows provide adequate separation - CHECK

⚠️ Potential Issues

  • 5-Star (Gold): Yellow stars on yellow/amber gradient may have low contrast
  • Bottom Areas: Light gradient areas (green, blue, pink, amber) may reduce white text contrast
  • Small Text: 11-12px text may be harder to read on lighter gradient sections

💡 Recommendations

  • Add stronger text shadows for text on light gradient areas
  • Consider using a darker overlay at the bottom of cards
  • For 5-star mods, consider white or outlined stars instead of yellow
  • Ensure badges have semi-transparent dark backgrounds
