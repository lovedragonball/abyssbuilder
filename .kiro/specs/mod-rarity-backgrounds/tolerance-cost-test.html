• Adjusted: bg-emerald-500/15 border border-emerald-400/70 text-emerald-200 shadow-[0_0_12px_rgba(16,185,129,0.6)]

Positioning:
• position: absolute; bottom: 8px; right: 8px;

• Font size: 10px
• Font weight: 700 (bold)
• Padding: 4px 8px

✅ Expected Results
Pass Criteria:
• All tolerance numbers are clearly readable
• Strikethrough text is distinguishable
• Badge backgrounds provide adequate contrast
• Emerald glow is visible on light backgrounds
• No overlapping with other UI elements

Potential Issues:
• Adjusted state glow may be too subtle on bright gradients
• Green badge on green gradient (2-star matched) may lack contrast
• Blue badge on blue gradient (3-star normal) may lack contrast
• Strikethrough text may be hard to read at small size
