# DUET NIGHT ABYSS DAMAGE CALCULATION FORMULA AND DEMON WEDGE MECHANICS

This document provides the core damage formula and explains the "Damage Bucket" system, which is crucial for accurately calculating the effect of Demon Wedge stats.

## 1. CORE DAMAGE FORMULA (Multiplicative Buckets)

The total damage is calculated by multiplying several independent "buckets" (multipliers) together.

```
Damage = 
    Base_DMG 
    * Enemy_DEF_Multiplier 
    * Enemy_RES_Multiplier 
    * Resolve_Multiplier 
    * Morale_Multiplier 
    * DMG_Boost_Multiplier 
    * DMG_Bonus_Multiplier 
    * CRIT_Multiplier 
    * Enemy_Damage_Taken_Multiplier 
    * Final_DMG_Multiplier 
    * RNG_Multiplier
```

---

## 2. DEMON WEDGE STAT APPLICATION (Additive Stacking within Buckets)

The primary function of Demon Wedge stats is to contribute to the values within these multiplicative buckets. **Stats of the same type are ADDITIVE (summed up) before being applied to their respective bucket.** This is the key to understanding Diminishing Returns.

### A. BASE DMG BUCKET

The Base Damage is the foundation of the calculation. Demon Wedge stats contribute to the `Scalar` and `MV Multiplier` components.

#### Base DMG Formula:
```
Base_DMG = MV_Multiplier * Skill_or_Weapon_Scalar
```

#### 1. ATK% (Demon Wedge Main/Sub Stat)
- **Bucket:** Contributes to the `Skill_or_Weapon_Scalar`.
- **Stacking:** **ADDITIVE** with all other ATK% sources (e.g., Trial Rank, other Demon Wedges).

**Formula for Character ATK Scalar (Used for Skill DMG):**
```
Skill_Scalar = Base_Character_ATK * (1 + SUM(All_ATK_Percent)) * (1 + SUM(All_Elemental_ATK_Percent))
```

**Formula for Weapon DMG Scalar (Used for Weapon/Consonance DMG):**
```
Weapon_Scalar = 
    [Character_Base_ATK * (1 + SUM(Char_ATK_Percent)) * (1 + SUM(Char_Elemental_ATK_Percent))] 
    + [Weapon_Base_ATK * (1 + SUM(Weapon_ATK_Percent)) * (1 + SUM(WeaponType_ATK_Percent)) * Proficiency]
```

#### 2. Skill DMG% (Demon Wedge Main/Sub Stat, e.g., Arbiter's Illusionary Sacrifice)
- **Bucket:** Contributes to the `MV_Multiplier`.
- **Stacking:** **ADDITIVE** with all other Skill DMG% sources.

**Formula for MV Multiplier:**
```
MV_Multiplier = Base_Skill_Multiplier * (1 + SUM(All_Skill_DMG_Percent))
```

### B. DMG BOOST MULTIPLIER BUCKET

This bucket is a major source of damage increase and is where general damage buffs are grouped.

- **Stats Included:** Any stat worded as: `DMG Boost`, `[Weapon Type] DMG Boost`, `Weapon DMG`, `Skill DMG`, or `"Increases Damage Dealt"`.
- **Stacking:** **ADDITIVE** with all other DMG Boost sources.

**Formula for DMG Boost Multiplier:**
```
DMG_Boost_Multiplier = 1 + SUM(All_Damage_Boost_Percent)
```

### C. CRIT MULTIPLIER BUCKET

This bucket determines the damage bonus on a critical hit.

#### CRIT Damage (Demon Wedge Main/Sub Stat, e.g., Cerberus's Rage)
- **Bucket:** Contributes to the `CRIT_Multiplier`.
- **Stacking:** **ADDITIVE** with all other CRIT Damage sources.

**Formula for CRIT Multiplier (if the attack crits):**
```
CRIT_Multiplier = 1 + CRIT_Level * (SUM(All_CRIT_Damage_Percent) - 1)
```
*Note: `CRIT_Level` is 1 by default, but increases if CRIT Rate exceeds 100%.*

## 3. SUMMARY FOR CALCULATOR IMPLEMENTATION

To accurately model the impact of a Demon Wedge, the calculator must:

1.  **Identify the Stat Type:** Determine if the Demon Wedge provides ATK%, Skill DMG%, CRIT Damage, or a general DMG Boost.
2.  **Sum the Bucket:** Add the Demon Wedge's percentage value to the total sum of all other stats in that specific bucket (e.g., `Total_ATK_Percent = Existing_ATK_Percent + New_Demon_Wedge_ATK_Percent`).
3.  **Apply Multiplicatively:** Use the final summed value of each bucket to calculate the total damage by multiplying the independent buckets together.

**Example of Multiplicative Stacking (Optimization Principle):**
-   **Bad Stacking (Additive):** Adding 50% ATK% to an existing 100% ATK% only results in a 50% relative gain to the ATK Scalar.
-   **Good Stacking (Multiplicative):** Adding 50% ATK% (to the Base DMG bucket) and 50% Skill DMG% (to the MV Multiplier bucket) results in a much larger overall damage increase because the two buckets multiply each other.

This structure ensures that the calculator accurately reflects the in-game mechanics, where spreading stats across different buckets is more efficient than stacking one type.

# DUET NIGHT ABYSS: DEMON WEDGE IMPACT ON DAMAGE CALCULATION (FOR AI COPILOT)

This document focuses exclusively on how Demon Wedge stats are integrated into the damage formula via the "Damage Bucket" system. The goal is to provide a clear, structured guide for calculating the marginal damage increase from adding a specific Demon Wedge.

## 1. CORE PRINCIPLE: ADDITIVE VS. MULTIPLICATIVE STACKING

The entire damage calculation is based on the principle of **Additive Stacking within Buckets** and **Multiplicative Stacking between Buckets**.

| Stacking Type | Principle | Effect on Demon Wedge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Additive** | Stats of the same type are summed together: `(Stat_A + Stat_B + Stat_C)` | Leads to **Diminishing Returns** when stacking the same stat (e.g., ATK%) from multiple Demon Wedges. |
| **Multiplicative** | Independent buckets are multiplied together: `Bucket_1 * Bucket_2 * Bucket_3` | Provides the **Highest Damage Gain** when Demon Wedge stats are spread across different buckets. |

## 2. DEMON WEDGE STAT BUCKETS

Demon Wedge stats fall into three primary damage buckets. The final value of each bucket is a multiplier in the overall damage formula.

### A. BASE DMG SCALAR BUCKET (ATK%, HP%, DEF%)

This bucket determines the raw power value that the skill/weapon multiplier (MV) is applied to.

| Demon Wedge Stat | Damage Type Affected | Calculation Principle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ATK%** | Skill Damage, Weapon Damage | **ADDITIVE** with all other ATK% sources (Character, Weapon, Trial Rank). |
| **HP%** | Skill Damage (if skill scales with HP) | **ADDITIVE** with all other HP% sources. |
| **DEF%** | Skill Damage (if skill scales with DEF) | **ADDITIVE** with all other DEF% sources. |

**Impact Calculation (Example: ATK%):**
1.  Calculate the total ATK% sum: $\sum \text{ATK}\% = \text{Base\_ATK}\% + \text{Wedge\_1\_ATK}\% + \text{Wedge\_2\_ATK}\% + \dots$
2.  The Scalar is proportional to: $(1 + \sum \text{ATK}\%)$

### B. MV MULTIPLIER BUCKET (Skill DMG%)

This bucket modifies the skill's Motion Value (MV) or base damage percentage.

| Demon Wedge Stat | Damage Type Affected | Calculation Principle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Skill DMG%** | Skill Damage, Weapon Damage | **ADDITIVE** with all other Skill DMG% sources. |

**Impact Calculation:**
1.  Calculate the total Skill DMG% sum: $\sum \text{Skill\_DMG}\% = \text{Base\_Skill\_DMG}\% + \text{Wedge\_1\_Skill\_DMG}\% + \dots$
2.  The MV Multiplier is proportional to: $(1 + \sum \text{Skill\_DMG}\%)$

### C. DMG BOOST MULTIPLIER BUCKET (DMG Boost, Weapon DMG, Skill DMG)

This is a separate, multiplicative bucket that groups all general damage increases.

| Demon Wedge Stat | Damage Type Affected | Calculation Principle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DMG Boost** (General) | Skill Damage, Weapon Damage | **ADDITIVE** with all other DMG Boost sources. |
| **Weapon DMG** | Weapon Damage ONLY | **ADDITIVE** with all other Weapon DMG and General DMG Boost sources. |
| **Skill DMG** | Skill Damage ONLY | **ADDITIVE** with all other Skill DMG and General DMG Boost sources. |

**Impact Calculation:**
1.  Calculate the total DMG Boost sum: $\sum \text{DMG\_Boost} = \text{Base\_DMG\_Boost} + \text{Wedge\_1\_DMG\_Boost} + \dots$
2.  The DMG Boost Multiplier is: $(1 + \sum \text{DMG\_Boost})$

### D. CRIT MULTIPLIER BUCKET (CRIT Damage)

This bucket applies only if the attack is a critical hit.

| Demon Wedge Stat | Damage Type Affected | Calculation Principle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CRIT Damage** | Weapon Damage ONLY (Skills cannot crit) | **ADDITIVE** with all other CRIT Damage sources. |

**Impact Calculation:**
1.  Calculate the total CRIT Damage sum: $\sum \text{CRIT\_DMG} = \text{Base\_CRIT\_DMG} + \text{Wedge\_1\_CRIT\_DMG} + \dots$
2.  The CRIT Multiplier is proportional to: $(\sum \text{CRIT\_DMG})$

## 3. CALCULATOR LOGIC SUMMARY

To demonstrate the impact of a Demon Wedge, the calculator must:

1.  **Identify the Wedge's Primary Stat:** (e.g., ATK%, Skill DMG%, CRIT Damage).
2.  **Calculate the Total Bucket Value:** Sum the new Demon Wedge stat with the existing total of that stat type.
3.  **Calculate the Marginal Gain:**
    *   **Old Damage:** $\text{Damage}_{\text{Old}} = \text{Bucket}_{\text{Old}} \times \text{Other\_Buckets}$
    *   **New Damage:** $\text{Damage}_{\text{New}} = \text{Bucket}_{\text{New}} \times \text{Other\_Buckets}$
    *   **Percentage Increase:** $(\text{Damage}_{\text{New}} / \text{Damage}_{\text{Old}}) - 1$

This approach clearly illustrates the **Diminishing Returns** of stacking the same stat (Additive) versus the high efficiency of spreading stats across different buckets (Multiplicative).
